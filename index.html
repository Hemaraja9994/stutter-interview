Practise the interview before the interview

Draw a question, think for 30 seconds, then answer out loud for up to 90. Record yourself if you like: the recording stays on this device. Afterwards, rate how it went. The goal is not to be fluent. It is to say what you want to say, in your own way.

Disclosure statement

Telling the interviewer early that you stutter, in a matter-of-fact way, can make the interview easier for both of you. Research suggests an informative statement is viewed more positively than an apologetic one.

Byrd CT et al. Clinical utility of self-disclosure for adults who stutter: apologetic versus informative statements. J Fluency Disord 2017;54:1–13.

You are not alone

Self-help groups for people who stammer meet across India, for example through The Indian Stammering Association (TISA). A speech-language pathologist can help you prepare techniques and plan disclosure.
